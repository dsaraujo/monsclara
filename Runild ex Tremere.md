**Runhilde (Runild) ex Tremere** is an established maga originally detailed in _The Lion and the Lily: The Normandy Tribunal_ (Chapter Five: Normandy, Covenant of Montverte, pp. 58–61), who recently re-entered the campaign's orbit in **Session 127 (Autumn 1246)**.


### 1. Canonical Profile & Background (_The Lion and the Lily_)

- **Covenant & Tribunal:** Montverte in the Normandy Tribunal. Montverte is a martial, raiding Autumn covenant perched in the forests near the Norman border[cite: 2, 6], ruled by an inner council that includes figures like Archmaga Geirlaug (Flambeau) and Sir Rotgiers de Gerberoy (Tremere).
    
      
    
- **Vital Statistics (as of canon baseline 1220 AD):**
    
      
    - Age: 64 (apparent age 43). In the saga's current timeframe (~1246–1249 AD), she is in her early 90s.
        
          
        
- **Personality & Demeanor:** _Oblivious to Mundanes +3, Catlike +2, Inquisitive +1_. Unlike the domineering, aggressive, or knightly personalities of her covenant sodales (such as Rotgiers), Runild is described as quiet, curious, and unassuming.
    
      
    
- **Role at Montverte:** She is the covenant’s librarian and archivist, responsible for overseeing Montverte’s magical resources: its library, vis stores, and the laboratory tithes/seasons of work donated by junior magi.
    
      
    
- **Familiar & Affinities:** Her familiar is a black cat descended from the lineage of Jerbiton’s familiar, and she associates closely with Montverte's numerous felines.
    
      
    
- **House Standing:** As of 1220, she had recently finished training her first apprentice but did not yet hold her own voting sigil (which in Normandy would be held up the hierarchy, likely by Rotgiers de Gerberoy).
    
      
    

### 2. Campaign Context & Current Narrative Continuity (_Mons Clara_)

- **Upcoming Visit (Spring 1249):** In **Session 127** (Autumn 1246), Leon ex Mercere delivered a missive to **Ravena** stating that **Runilde ex Tremere plans to visit Mons Clara in the Spring of 1249**.
    
      
    
- **Saga Dynamics & Story Potential for Ravena & Mons Clara:**
    
      
    - **House Tremere Lineage & Politics:** Ravena is the resident Tremere of Mons Clara. While Ravena has struggled with the rigid, austere expectations of Coeris (as reflected in her dream-journeys), Runhilde offers a contrasting face of the House—scholarly, bookish, and focused on stewardship rather than military command or certamen dominance.
        
          
        
    - **Normandy/French Politics:** Montverte is deeply entangled in French northern politics and chivalric border raiding. With Prince/Count Charles d’Anjou expanding northern Capetian control into Provence (notably marrying Beatrice of Provence), visitors from Normandy carry significant political resonance.
        
          
        
    - **Library & Resource Exchange:** Given Runild’s official office as keeper of Montverte’s library and vis vaults, her visit is an ideal narrative vehicle for high-level book exchanges, tractatus trades, or inquiries into lost northern lab texts and Hermetic lore.
