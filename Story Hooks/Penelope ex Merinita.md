## Fairy Legends

**The Gévaudan**

Between 1764-1767, a mysterious wolf-like creature terrorized the region of Gévaudan. Accounts are brutal, describing numerous deaths, particularly children. The Beast became infamous, inspiring widespread fear and even intervention from the King of France. Its true nature remains debated - a monstrous wolf, a hybrid animal, or a trained killer?

**The Tarasque of Tarascon**

Summary: A dragon-like monster terrorized the town of Tarascon. St. Martha tamed the Tarasque with prayers and holy water. The creature became docile and was led back to the town where, in fear, the people killed it.

Story Potential: Modern-day twist! What if the Tarasque wasn't killed, just dormant? Perhaps construction in the area disturbs its lair. Or, could a reclusive descendant of the Tarasque exist, struggling with their nature against the backdrop of a summer festival celebrating the legend?

**The Drac**

The Legend: The Drac is a mischievous water spirit found throughout Occitan folklore. It can shapeshift, cause floods, lure children to rivers and play tricks with its power over water. It's often depicted as part-man, part-fish, sometimes even taking on draconic traits.

**The Stuff of Dreams**

Dreams are qualitatively different from other mental states; while desire, sadness, or madness are clearly related to the humors as direct expressions of Mentem, the content of dreams is always the stuff of sensation, and therefore Imaginem. Hence to create, enter, or change a dream from outside one uses Mentem, but to enact changes within the dream one uses Imaginem.

Imaginem, not Mentem, is the success in the Dream world.

A magus preparing for dream travel may choose to extend his Parma Magica to cover his body and spirit as separate entities (using the rules in ArM5, page 66), reducing his effective Parma score by 3.

## The Script: "The Shattered Gate of Morpheus"

Because Somnifer is trapped in a distant regio, he cannot act as a physical Mystagogue[cite: 1, 6]. This requires an experimental, purely oneiric version of a self-initiation/assisted script. It uses **Imaginem** as its core alignment, as dictated by the _Volshebnii Mechtateli_[cite: 5, 6].
- **Initiation Ease Factor:** 15 (Minor Mystery Virtue known to the Mystagogue).
- **Script Bonus Required:** Somnifer has Presence 0 and _Volshebnii Mechtateli Lore_ 5 (Total 5). He needs a **Script Bonus of +10** to guarantee success without a die roll.
### Script Components & Bonuses (+13 Total)
- **Special Time (+3):** The initiation must take place during the **Morsure du Soleil** (the exact moment of a solar eclipse or a perfectly dark, moonless night of the Winter Solstice).
- **Major Ordeal (+9):** Penelope must take on a permanent flaw carved into her psyche. Given her existing narrative connection (her harrowing nightmare in 1223 that gave her a fear of childbirth), she undergoes a **Medium Ordeal** that upgrades her _Fear of Childbirth_ into a **Major Personality Flaw: Driven (To Protect and Foster Lost Souls/Children of Dream)** (+6 Script Bonus) OR she takes on a **Major Hermetic Flaw: Restriction (Cannot cast magic during the waking hours if she has not slept/dreamed the night before)** (+9 Script Bonus). Let us go with the latter to emphasize her drifting closer to the dreaming realm.
- **The Symbolic Sacrifice (+1):** Penelope must sacrifice a highly prized waking memory. She completely forgets her very first memory of her birth family or her mundane childhood, leaving a smooth, silent void in her past.
## The Three Ordeals (The Oneiric Trial)

Since this takes place entirely within a shared dreamscape, the trial is divided into three distinct movements, echoing the shifting domains of _The Sandman_[cite: 1, 6].

### Act I: The Parliament of Rooks (The Gathering of Senses)

Penelope falls into a deep, drug-induced sleep at _Mons Clara_ at the designated astrological hour[cite: 5, 6]. She awakens not in her room, but walking along a beach of black sand next to a sea of liquid mercury—the shores of Somnifer's trapped consciousness.

A flock of white rooks with human eyes descends. They do not speak; they whisper her forgotten thoughts back to her. Somnifer appears, but he does not look like a normal magus. He is a tall, emaciated figure wrapped in a cloak made of shifting twilight, his eyes glowing like rubies in the dark (resembling Dream of the Endless)[cite: 1, 6].

> **The Mystagogue's Challenge:**
> 
> _"To pull the waking world into the dream is an act of supreme hubris. To pull the dream into the waking world is a crime against reality. What will you offer to weigh down your phantom steps?"_

Penelope must actively use **Intelligence + Dream Magic** to reshape the mercury sea into a solid staircase of glass. She must step onto it, willingly relinquishing her most cherished mundane memory to Somnifer's rooks. As she does, the rooks devour it, and the staircase turns to cold, heavy iron under her feet.
### Act II: The Corridor of Lost Hopes (The Confrontation)

Somnifer leads her through a corridor flanked by thousands of mirrors—but none of them reflect her face. They reflect the dreams of the people currently sleeping in the Provençal Tribunal: her fellow sodales at _Mons Clara_, the terrified Cathar heretics hiding in the hills, and the crusaders polishing their iron armor.

At the end of the hall stands a manifestation of her own sub-conscious nightmare. It is a massive, shifting entity made of thorned vines and unhatched stone eggs (playing on her fear of births/creations gone wrong).

To pass, Penelope cannot simply blast it with _Perdo_ or _Rego_. She must use **The Greater Dream Grimoire's** philosophy: she must _embrace_ the nightmare, realizing it has no power unless she feeds it attention[cite: 1, 6]. She must perform an act of **Lucid Dreaming** (Intelligence vs. Ease Factor 9) to transform the thorned monster into a harmless, weeping child made of glass.
### Act III: The Forging of the Ivory Gate

Somnifer hands her the glass child.

> _"Give it substance,"_ he commands. _"Let its first breath be drawn in the waking air."_

This is the mechanical climax. Within the dream, Penelope must spontaneously enact the new **Creo (Form) Level 15 Mystery Ritual** to give a dream object physical substance in the real world. She must draw upon the Arcane Connection to her physical body back at Mons Clara to channel real-world _Creo_ or _Imaginem_ vis sitting in her laboratory[cite: 5, 6].

As she channels the vis, her dreaming hands begin to bleed real, hot blood. The glass child melts, reshaping itself into a heavy, intricately carved key made of unmelting ice and ancient ivory. The sheer shock of the real-world vis crossing into the dreamscape snaps the initiation complete, waking her instantly.
## The Aftermath at Mons Clara

When Penelope opens her eyes in her sanctum at _Mons Clara_:

1. **The Physical Token:** Clutching tightly in her physical hand is the **Ivory Key** she forged in the dream. Thanks to _The Greater Dream Grimoire_, she has successfully pulled an item from the Dreaming into reality for the first time. It acts as a permanent Arcane Connection to Somnifer's pocket regio in Fontainebleau[cite: 1, 6].
2. **The Flaw Manifests:** Her eyes now take on a subtle, starry, or dilated quality whenever the sun sets, and she feels an absolute, heavy exhaustion if she tries to practice magic during the day without having spent the night deep in the dreamlands (her new Ordeal Flaw).
## Penelope's Progression in the Volshebnii Mechtateli

As she steps into the rank of the **Monarchs of the Veiled Court**, her role in the cult and the saga expands drastically:

- **The Dream Network:** She can now physically step into the dreams of her sodales or nearby mundanes. This is an incredible tool for investigating the political machinations of **Ahmad ibn Nasr** or the factional wars of **Tolosa Paratge** without tripping the automated scrying laws of the Quaesitors (since dream-magic operates entirely under _Imaginem_ and skips standard Mentem scrying detection)[cite: 3, 5, 6].
- **The Sandbox Hook:** Because she can pull objects out of dreams, you can introduce bizarre, surreal _Sandman_-esque plot devices[cite: 1, 6]. A covenfolk dreams of a lost Visigothic treasure map; Penelope can physically go into their dream, find the map, and bring it back to the waking world[cite: 1, 6].
