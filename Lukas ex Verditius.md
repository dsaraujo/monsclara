Lukas was gauntleted at the grand covenant of Fengheld in the Rhine Tribunal[cite: 3, 6]. While his master pushed him toward the traditional Rhine focus on heavy forge-craft and weaponry, Lukas became utterly infatuated with the sky. He successfully initiated into **Planetary Magic** through a minor astrological sodality in the north, but his ambitions were instantly stonewalled. The Hierophants of his local circle refused to teach him the higher mysteries of **Celestial Magic** without a decade of political servitude—a delay his Verditius Hubris simply could not brook.

Hearing wild rumors through the Redcap network of a Verditius in the distant, war-torn south who mathematically mapped a total solar eclipse and successfully synthesized star-charts into a forge talismans (_Ornatus Zodiacus_), Lukas packed his casting tools and left the Rhine entirely[cite: 5, 6]. He is currently traveling through Arelat, looking for a master willing to trade the secrets of the Spheres for pristine Rhine lab texts and flawless craftsmanship[cite: 1, 6].

## Letter to Spatha from Lukas ex Verditius

> **To the Most Illustrious and Star-Wise Artisan, Spatha of House Verditius,**
> 
> May the light of the ethereal spheres guide your hammer, and may Mars never find your forge misaligned.
> 
> I pray you forgive this unannounced intrusion by ink, sent across borders via the loyal network of House Mercere. My name is Lukas, filius of a traditional forge-master of Fengheld within the Rhine Tribunal. Though my birth-tribunal is rich in wood and stone, it is utterly destitute of vision. My step-brethren spend their seasons hammering cold iron, completely blind to the perfect clockwork of the cosmos turning above their smoke-filled laboratories.
> 
> My own eyes have ever been fixed on the macrocosm. I have successfully walked the initial path of **Planetary Magic**, learning to adjust my casting tools to the alignments of the seven errant stars. Yet, when I sought to advance my craft into the true mysteries of **Celestial Magic**—to permanently bind the ruling signs and houses into items of quality—the insular Hierophants of the north demanded years of political fealty before unlocking their scripts.
> 
> I chose exile instead. If I am to offer my labor and my deference to a Master, it will be to one whose work has echoed across the Alps. Word has reached me through the _paradoxographia_ of traveling Jerbiton of your unmatched astronomical focus at Mons Clara. They whisper of your _Sinum Via Lactea_, and your flawless engineering during the great solar blackness of 1235. To find a sodalis who does not merely watch the stars, but weaves their numeric patterns into the very metal of his talismans, has given me a new North Star to follow.
> 
> I am currently journeying down the Rhone Valley into the Provençal Tribunal. I carry with me several pristine laboratory texts from the scriptoriums of Fengheld, alongside rare minerals harvested from the Black Forest. I do not seek a handout, Sodalis. I seek a true Mystagogue. I am willing to trade years of meticulous laboratory assistance, precise craft-cooperation, or my finest northern secrets in exchange for your guidance across the threshold of the Higher Astrological Arts.
> 
> I shall await your response at the Mercer House of the Coenobium. Let me know if the portents favor my approach to your mountain.
> 
> **In absolute reverence of your craft,**
> 
> **Lukas ex Verditius**
> _Ambulator of House Verditius_

### Storyguide Collaboration Ideas for Session 108+

Given the current high-stakes tension in your saga—with **Stheno the Gorgon** released, a 4-meter **Bronze Angel** petrifying Spatha's soldiers, and the impending _Wizards' War_ friction with **Tres**—Lukas's arrival can serve several brilliant narrative purposes:

1. **The Core Theory Solution:** Spatha is currently trying to figure out how to handle the Bronze Angel and the strange, un-Hermetic protections it expands. Lukas, coming from the highly theoretical circles of Fengheld, might possess a piece of astrological lore or a unique Rhine text regarding _Empyreal Auras_ or Divine constructs that helps Spatha analyze the Angel's specific "order of protection."[cite: 5, 6]
    
2. **The Forge Assistant:** Spatha's lab schedule is slammed. If he takes Lukas on as an aspirant/assistant in exchange for a future _Celestial Magic_ script initiation, Lukas can grant Spatha a massive bonus in the lab via House cooperation, freeing Spatha up to invent the Perdo Vim counter-magics needed to reverse Stheno's petrification[cite: 5, 6].
