### On the Nature and Secrets of the Gorgons

- **The Blood of Duality (The Asclepian Secret):** The ancient myth is literal truth, codified by the first theurgists of our sanctuary[cite: 3, 16]. The blood taken from a Gorgon's veins possesses absolute polarity:
    
      
    - _The Right Flank:_ Flows with supreme restorative virtue (potent _Creo Corpus_), capable of purging any poison, healing shattered organs, or augmenting Longevity workings beyond mortal limits[cite: 3, 6]. It is this very ichor that Athena granted to Asclepius to raise the dead.
        
          
        
    - _The Left Flank:_ A virulent, corrosive venom (destructive _Perdo Corpus/Vim_) that dissolves mortal flesh, rots enchantments, and curdles the blood of men and beasts in mere moments[cite: 3, 6].
        
          
        
- **The Immortality of the Elder Sisters:**
    
    Unlike Medusa, who was mortal and fell to Perseus’s scythe, **Stheno** and **Euryale** are immortal embodiments of the primordial wild[cite: 3, 6]. They cannot be slain by conventional Hermetic violence; if discorporated by raw force, their spirit re-coagulates within the deep chthonic earth or its nearest magical tether[cite: 3, 6]. They must be appeased, bound by metaphysical pacts, or warded through specific symbolic counter-charms[cite: 3, 8].
    
      
    
- **The Petrifaction & The Gorgoneion:**
    
    A Gorgon’s gaze does not merely harden the flesh—it drags the vital soul into the stillness of elemental stone (_Terram/Mentem_)[cite: 3, 6].
    
      
    - To gaze into her eyes directly bypasses ordinary Hermetic defenses unless shielded by an unbroken _Parma Magica_ fortified against _Terram_ or a reflective barrier[cite: 3, 6].
        
          
        
    - A mirror or polished bronze shield (such as the archetype of Athena) diverts the basilisk-glare into harmless visual feedback[cite: 3, 6].
        
          
        
    - The severed countenance or a shed mask of a Gorgon—the true _Gorgoneion_—is the ultimate defensive talisman, causing terror in all encroaching spirits and warding against lesser supernatural predators.
        
          
        

### What I Can Offer in Exchange

Should your covenant wish to treat with her safely—or harness the arcane spoils of having an immortal sister on your doorstep—I propose an exchange of sodality:

  

1. **The Compendium of Chthonic Anatomy (_De Natura Gorgonis_):** A tractatus on _Magic Lore_ (Quality 11) detailing the harvest of shedding scales, venom extraction, and the celestial alignments (the rising of _Algol_, the Demon Star) under which Gorgon-tainted magic fluctuates.
    
      
    
2. **Lab Text: _The Mirrored Veil of Athena_ (_ReIm / ReTe 25_):** An enchantment formula designed for armor, visors, or shields that bends gaze-attacks away from the wearer and grants complete immunity to sudden optical curses and petrification.
    
      
    
3. **Restorative Elixir Scripts:**
    
    Methods to stabilize and refine raw Gorgon venom into pure _Corpus_ or _Perdo_ vis without suffering accidental warping or venomous corruption[cite: 3, 6].
    
      
    

### What I Request from Mons Clara

In return for these texts and my ongoing counsel:

  

- **Arcane Samples:** Three vials of naturally shed venom or water from the pool where Stheno bathes her serpent locks, along with two unblemished silver or bronze scale-sheds.
    
      
    
- **A Transcribed Rubbing:** An exact tracing of the archaic Greek inscriptions carved into the bronze doors of Julius ex Merinita’s subterranean prison.
    
      
    
- **Astronomical Observations:** A copy of your stellar charts tracking the occultation of _Algol_ as viewed from your high mountain observatory.
